# Solo Leveling Dungeon Hunter

A Solo Leveling dungeon hunter with a Summoners War feel. Start on Monarch Island, pick a gate on the world map, and clear its 10 stages — Easy, Hard, and Hell — with a 4-shadow team. No build step — open `index.html` in any modern browser. It also installs as a free app on your phone (see "Install it as an app" below).

## How it plays

- **Monarch Island home base.** A floating island at night — moon, drifting clouds, floating rock shards, glowing mana crystals, a summoning ring, and a manafall off the cliff. Your hunter and battle team stand on it, by an element-tinted portal.
- **Floating icon dock.** Hunt, Map, Shop, Army, and Level Up are floating icons — a pill of round icons at the bottom on tall screens, a vertical rail on the right edge when your phone is sideways. No tab bar crowding the world.
- **A clean Hunt page.** The world area is big and full-width. There are no player stats at the top and no battle log — just the island and the trail.
- A short summary pops up as a System Notification when the game starts, then fades away on its own (or tap START HUNTING). The title is a small chip in the top-left.
- **Landscape-first.** The game is built to be played sideways on a phone — the whole Hunt screen fits with no page scroll, and battles open as pop-up modals over the map instead of scrolling. A rotate hint shows if you're holding the phone upright.

## Expedition Trail — no more square grid

- Each dungeon is a painted **Expedition Trail**, not a grid of squares: monsters, chests, and the boss stand along a glowing winding path tinted by the gate's element.
- Tap a monster, chest, or the boss and your hunter walks over to fight it, loot it, or take the portal. Arrow keys and WASD still work. There is no D-pad anymore.
- Old saves carry over onto the trail automatically.

## World map — 10 gates

- The map is painted with a dotted route and glowing portal nodes for all 10 element-themed gates: **Embers (Fire), Frost (Water), Thorns (Earth), Echoes (Wind), Ash (Dark), Deep (Water), Storms (Wind), Demon Castle (Dark), Monarch (Light), Chaos (Fire)**.
- Tap a gate and you get named stage rows with enemy previews and a **BATTLE** button on each one.
- Hitting BATTLE opens a **VS screen** — your hunter and 4-shadow team on one side, the stage's horde and boss on the other, your title bonus up top — with START BATTLE and Cancel.
- Each gate has **Easy, Hard, and Hell** modes — Hard is about twice as strong with better rewards, Hell about three times. Each mode has **10 stages**, with a boss finale.
- Clear all 10 stages on Easy to **conquer the gate** — that earns the gate's title and unlocks the next gate and Hard mode. Clear Hard to open Hell.
- Cleared stages can be **re-run forever** to farm XP, gold, and themed runes. Each gate's monsters, boss, and rune drops match its element.
- Battles field a **4-shadow team** you pick in the Army pop-up. The rest of your army waits in reserve.

## Battles — 3D battlefield

- **Your team on your side, backs to you.** Your hunter and shadows stand large in the foreground with their backs to you, rim-lit on glowing element rings. The enemies stand far up-field facing your team, smaller with distance, with HP plates floating over them.
- Combat is turn-by-turn, based on speed: everyone has their own turn gauge.
- **You pick each monster's attack.** In manual mode, when one of your shadows' turns comes up, the battle holds, that shadow glows gold, and a YOUR MOVE tray pops up with its 4 attacks and their cooldowns. You choose. AUTO still picks for you, and flipping AUTO on mid-prompt hands the turn to the AI.
- Attack, or use the hunter's skills: Power Strike, Shadow Rush, and Monarch's Wrath.
- AUTO battle and ×1/×2/×3 speed, plus a Flee button. Drinking a potion still works inside battle, and auto-battle still drinks at low HP.
- Beat a boss and you can ARISE it as a shadow — each shadow adds a permanent damage bonus.

## Summons (Shadow Army)

- Mystic Scroll summons build a collectible Shadow Army that fights with you. The roster is an officer corps — soldiers, knights, captains, and generals, plus liches and the Lich King, angels and the Archangel, demons and the Demon Lord. No filler.
- Each summon has one of 6 elements — **Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Light, Dark** — with an advantage cycle, and one of 4 roles: **Attack, Defense, Healer, Support**.
- Each summon has **4 named skills with descriptions**, so you can see what it's good at and build toward that. Attacks run off each summon's own skills, element, and role.
- Summons level up from their own attacks — XP for every hit they land, plus a bonus for the killing blow.
- Each summon can **evolve twice, at Lv 8 and Lv 16, and each evolution doubles its total power**.
- Each summon has **6 rune slots**. Runes come in 5 sets (Fatal, Energy, Guard, Rage, Swift) and drop from monsters and bosses — themed to the gate you cleared them in.
- Summons look smooth and glossy now — big elemental portraits with glow, no pixelated sprites.

## Hunter customization

- Customize your hunter in the Level Up pop-up: 6 armor styles, 7 armor colors, 5 headgear options, and 6 auras. Your look shows on the island and in battle.
- The Shop sells 4 weapon types — **daggers, swords, spears, and scythes** — each with its own damage, speed, and crit profile. Spears unlock after 2 gates cleared, scythes after 4. Six weapon skins rename and restyle your equipped weapon.
- **Titles are earned, one per gate cleared** — from Gate Breaker up to True Monarch at gate 10. Each title grants its own bonus (gold, XP, damage, defense, or speed), live in your stats the moment you equip it.

## Shop — potions live here now

- A shop sells weapons, armor, and healing. **Potions are bought in the Shop only (60 G, stock shown)** — there are no potion buttons on the main Hunt screen. Drops and chest potions are untouched.

## Progression

- Earn XP and gold, level up from Rank E toward S and Monarch, and spend stat points on Strength, Agility, and Vitality.
- Leveling up opens a pop-up — spend your stat points or keep hunting.
- Tap any summon card in the Army pop-up to jump straight to that summon on the Level Up page — its card flashes gold so you spot it.

## Install it as an app — free, no store

- It's an installable web app. Tap the **Install App** button in the header: on Android it fires the real install prompt; on iPhone it walks you through Share → Add to Home Screen.
- It launches fullscreen from your home screen with the violet-gate icon, no browser chrome, and works offline once installed.
- Updates still flow through like always — the app checks for the newest version every time you open it while online. Your save carries straight over through every update.

## Saving

Progress auto-saves after every action and when you close the tab — level, XP, gold, shadow army, runes, gates, and your exact spot. Come back and it picks up right where you left off. A "Progress saved" line shows the last save time. Restart clears the save. Old saves carry over — gates you already passed count as conquered on Easy.

## Controls

- Move: tap a monster, chest, or boss on the trail to walk over to it, or use arrow keys / WASD.
- Battle: on your shadow's turn, pick one of its 4 attacks from the YOUR MOVE tray; tap a skill, a potion, or Flee (or press Esc to flee). AUTO picks for you.
- Army: tap a summon card to open it on the Level Up tab.
