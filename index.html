    🎨 HUNTER CUSTOMIZATION

    🏅 TITLES — EARNED BY CLEARING GATES

    Every gate boss you bring down earns a new title. Equip one title at a time — each carries its own bonus to damage, speed, gold, XP, or defense.

    ◆ YOUR RUNES — 6 SLOTS

    Monsters drop runes — bosses always drop one. Tap a rune to inscribe it, tap an inscribed slot to remove it. Fatal +damage · Energy +HP · Guard damage cut · Rage +crit · Swift +shadow damage. Your shadows carry 6 rune slots of their own — inscribe theirs in Shadow Growth below.

    👤 SHADOW GROWTH — LEVELS & RUNES

    Shadows gain XP every time they attack in battle — plus a bonus for a killing blow. Each shadow has 6 rune slots and 4 skills shaped by its element and role: Attack shadows burst, Defense shadows shield you, Healers restore your HP, Support shadows buff the team. Evolve twice — at Lv 8 and Lv 16 — and each evolution doubles that shadow's power (×2, then ×4). On a shadow, Fatal · Energy · Guard · Swift add damage, Rage adds crit — so match runes to the role's strengths listed on its card.
